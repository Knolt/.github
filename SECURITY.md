# Security policy

Knolt runs next to security cameras, so we take reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't open a public issue. Instead:

- use **Report a vulnerability** on the affected repository's **Security** tab, or
- contact us privately through [knolt.ca](https://knolt.ca).

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it could expose. We'll acknowledge your report within 3 business days and keep you updated until it's fixed.

## Scope

Knolt's code, its on-site box and the services it runs. Please don't access footage or data that isn't yours, disrupt a running system, or test against a customer's site.
